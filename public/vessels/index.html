WHAT IS INCLUDED

Names, stations and source records

RNLI station allocations, independent lifeboats, coastguard craft, and individually documented police and fire boats. Source links are retained for each record.

WHAT MMSI MEANS

Identity and AIS are different

Official radio registers can confirm an MMSI without confirming that a boat transmits AIS. Public AIS references are labelled separately. A lookup may have no current position.

KNOWN LIMITS

Small boats leave gaps

Some inflatable, reserve and unnamed boats have no published identifier. Fleet assignments change. This is a public-reference snapshot; it does not represent live deployments.

Source notes, unresolved records and attribution
